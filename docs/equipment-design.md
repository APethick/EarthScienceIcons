# Equipment design references

These are schematic vector compositions, not manufacturer logos or engineering drawings. Receiver housings are yellow with black outlines to follow the library convention.

- Geological pick: flat striking face, forged neck, curved tapering pick and moulded grip. [Estwing pointed-tip rock pick](https://www.estwing.com/product/rock-pick-pointed-tip/).
- Conductivity meters: long EM31 boom versus compact EM38 enclosure. [Geonics conductivity meters](https://geonics.com/html/conductivitymeters.html).
- TEMPEST: fixed-wing transmitter loop with separate towed receiver. [Goyder Institute AEM system report](https://goyderinstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/goyder_trs_18-6_musgrave_processing_regional_aem.pdf).
- [Betsy Gun seismic source](https://betsygun.com/).
- [VPEG eVib electric vibrator](https://rtclark.com/product/vpeg-evib-electromagnetic-seismic-vibrator/).
- [INOVA Quantum node](https://inova3.frb.io/products/quantum).
- [SmartSolo IGU-16HR](https://smartsolo.com/sc/IGU-16HR.pdf).
- [Sercel WiNG](https://www.sercel.com/en/products/wing).
- [Geospace land acquisition equipment](https://www.geospace.com/products/land-exploration/).

The scale suite is qualitative, with unlabelled scale bars: pore, mineral grain, hand specimen, outcrop, structure, deposit, district and crust. The EM smoke ring depicts expanding induced currents after transmitter shutoff, not literal smoke. The loop-field symbol depicts closed magnetic flux with an axial field through the loop.
